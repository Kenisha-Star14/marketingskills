What to do next

Written from this fortnight’s numbers, not generic advice.

Instagram is breaking out — feed it

Account reach quadrupled this week (518 → 2,105) on the strength of the science reels: frozen shoulder and LIFTMOR each reached ~1,150+, five times your usual. The algorithm is testing you with a wider audience right now.

Do: hold a daily reel cadence for the next 2 weeks — momentum decays fast once you stop. The Jul 5–8 gap shows it: those four days were your lowest-reach days of the fortnight.

Evidence: IG views +304% w/w · followers +7 · reach on science reels 1,157 and 1,172 vs ~50–200 typical

TikTok wants your story, not your citations

Week 1 (personal story arc) averaged ~7.7% engagement and 136 interactions. Week 2 (research explainers) averaged ~2.3% and 23. Same account, same cadence — the content mix is the variable.

Do: on TikTok, open with the personal hook (“I was medicated for a disease I never had”) and push the study citation to the caption or a pinned comment. Keep the citation-forward edit for Instagram, where it’s winning reach.

Evidence: TT interactions 136 → 23 (-83%) · best TT post = thyroid story, 592 views, 42 likes

Convert the new Instagram reach into followers

2,105 people reached this week converted to just 7 new followers (~0.3%). The content earns the view; the profile isn’t closing.

Do: add a spoken “follow for the next study” line in the last 2 seconds of each reel, and make the bio lead with the outcome (“evidence-based strength coaching for perimenopause”) rather than the brand name.

Evidence: reach 2,105 → +7 followers · TikTok converts ~3× better (23 interactions → +8 followers)

Facebook: keep cross-posting, invest nothing more

Distribution is real (~1,800 video views/week) but inert — 7 interactions all week and zero follower growth. The exception: the creatine video did its best numbers here (519 views), suggesting the FB audience skews toward supplement and bone-density topics.

Do: keep the auto-crosspost. When you have supplement/bone-health content, let Facebook be its home.

Evidence: FB followers 152 flat · views 1,856 → 1,834 · interactions 25 → 7

Threads: engage, don’t just broadcast

Daily posting is not being distributed — most posts get under 20 views, and the “comment GUIDE” CTA posts get essentially none. The two that traveled (ferritin, 316 views; the tea confession, 192) were practical or personal, not promotional.

Do: swap 3 of the 7 weekly posts for 10 minutes of replying to large women’s-health accounts — replies are how Threads distributes new accounts. Keep the protocol-style posts.

Evidence: 2 followers · weekly views 652 → 192 · CTA posts ~ 0 views

YouTube Shorts is free reach you’re leaving off

X (2 followers, one tweet, ~0 impressions) and YouTube (1 subscriber, no uploads) are dormant. X can stay parked. YouTube can’t: you already produce a vertical video every day, and Shorts is the only one of these surfaces where content keeps earning search views for months.

Do: add YouTube Shorts to the same Metricool cross-post. Zero new production, evergreen upside.

Evidence: YT 3 video views in 14 days with no uploads · X followers 3 → 2

Written from this fortnight’s numbers, not generic advice.

402 followers — still growing during the pause

People are still finding you and choosing to stay while you rest — the clearest possible proof the foundation is real.

Evidence: audience 402 as of Aug 1 · IG 111 and TikTok 133, both records · gained through an 18-day posting pause

The Jul 25 dip fully reversed and then some: +1 on Jul 26, +1 on Jul 28, and the record 133 is holding. Someone is still finding the Oprah and story posts organically. Eighteen days without a new video is the only thing capping this — TikTok rewards returns fast.

Evidence: TT followers 131 → 133 with zero posts · no new video in 18 days · interactions flat at 0

Turn the milestone into the restart’s follow CTA

The quiet-week pattern held to the end: the people who found you during the pause followed at ten times the spike-week rate, and they delivered #400, #401 and #402. That story — “women keep finding this account even when I’m quiet, because the science holds up” — is itself the best follow CTA you have.

Do: bake it into the restart: a spoken “join 400+ smart women” in the last 2 seconds of each reel, and a bio that leads with the outcome (“evidence-based strength coaching for perimenopause”).

Evidence: conversion ~0.2% during spike weeks vs ~2%+ in quiet weeks · audience 402

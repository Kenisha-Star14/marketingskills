What to do next

Written from this fortnight’s numbers, not generic advice.

408 followers — seven weeks of near-silent growth

Follower #400 came in on Jul 28; since then every platform has contributed: Instagram holding at 115, Facebook at 154, Threads up to 4 (gained 1 this week), and TikTok at 132 — all earned by a single post circulating on its own for seven weeks. The organic growth continues even as the silence lengthens, which is rare and valuable data.

Do: the comeback post writes itself: “408+ smart women found this account while I was silent — here’s what we do here.” One reel, cross-posted everywhere, with special emphasis on Threads since it just proved it can move followers. If filming is the blocker, re-share the thyroid story or the Oprah reels through Metricool.

Evidence: audience 408 · Threads +1 this week, TikTok 132, IG 115 · gained through a 40-day posting pause

TikTok: peaked at a record 135 — earned while dark

The Jul 25 dip fully reversed and then some: climbed to a record 135 before settling back to 134. Someone is still finding the Oprah and story posts organically six weeks later. The only thing capping this growth is the absence of new content — TikTok rewards returns fast, and your back catalog is still working.

Do: TikTok first whenever the restart happens — a cultural-moment post if one exists this week, otherwise the personal story or the 410-milestone reel. Any proven format beats another dark day.

Evidence: TT followers held 131 → 134 net with zero posts · no new video in 37 days · interactions flat at 0

Turn the milestone into the restart’s follow CTA

The quiet-week pattern held steady: the people who found you during the pause have been following at a steady organic rate. They delivered followers #400 through #410. That story — “women keep finding this account even when I’m quiet, because the science holds up” — is itself the best follow CTA you have.

Do: bake it into the restart: a spoken “join 410+ smart women” in the last 2 seconds of each reel, and a bio that leads with the outcome (“evidence-based strength coaching for perimenopause”).

Evidence: conversion ~0.2% during spike weeks vs ~2%+ in quiet weeks · audience 410

Facebook: holding steady at 155 followers

The page has held 155 followers through the pause, with the old-video trickle slowing gradually (18 → 15 views this week). The creatine (523) and achy-knees (379) videos remain its best ever, still earning views weeks after posting.

Do: keep the auto-crosspost exactly as is — when the restart happens, Facebook comes along for free. Supplement and joint-health topics are what travel here.

Evidence: FB followers holding at 155 · views 18 → 15 · interactions flat at 0

Threads: first sign of life — gained a follower this week

The account grew from 3 to 4 followers this week, and Threads recorded its first engagement since the pause began. This is the first movement on this platform in seven weeks, which suggests either someone discovered old posts or the algorithm is starting to test the account again. Before the pause, daily posting wasn’t being distributed — most posts got under 20 views, and promotional CTAs got essentially none.

Do: restart with Threads in the first batch of posts. Lead with personal or practical content (not sales), then 10 minutes replying to large women’s-health accounts — replies are how Threads distributes new accounts.

Evidence: 3 → 4 followers this week · first engagement recorded in 40 days · no posts since Jul 19

YouTube Shorts is free reach you’re leaving off

X (2 followers, one tweet, ~0 impressions) and YouTube (1 subscriber, no uploads) are dormant. X can stay parked. YouTube can’t: you already produce a vertical video every day when active, and Shorts is the only one of these surfaces where content keeps earning search views for months after posting.

Do: when you restart, add YouTube Shorts to the same Metricool cross-post. Zero new production, evergreen upside.

Evidence: YT 2 video views in 14 days with no uploads · X followers flat at 2

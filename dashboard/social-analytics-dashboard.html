What to do next

Written from this fortnight’s numbers, not generic advice.

The drought is now the whole story — and growth just stalled

Six days with only one photo post, and the old content’s tail is exhausted: daily Instagram reach is down to single digits, and the follower count sat still at 398 for the first time since tracking began. Two followers from 400, the engine needs one post to restart.

Do: one reel today, cross-posted everywhere. It genuinely does not need to be perfect — a 30-second talking-to-camera on the personal story (“my own knees at 48”) restarts distribution. The Jul 9 restart doubled reach within two days.

Evidence: audience flat at 398 since Jul 21 · IG daily reach 4 on Jul 21 · one post in six days

TikTok: a full week dark — the cushion is gone

Seven days without a new video, interactions down 92% (86 → 7), and zero follower movement this week — the Oprah post’s tail has fully wound down. TikTok rewards recency more than any platform you’re on, and it was your steadiest grower two weeks ago.

Do: TikTok first in today’s restart — a cultural-moment post if one exists this week, otherwise the personal story. Either proven format beats another dark day.

Evidence: TT interactions 86 → 7 · no new video since Jul 17 · followers flat at 132 after weeks of growth

The 400 milestone is one good post away

Quiet-week conversion has been remarkable throughout the drought — the handful of people still finding you keep following at rates ten times the spike weeks. At 398, the next two followers arrive with the next post that reaches anyone at all.

Do: bake the follow CTA into the restart: a spoken “follow for the next study” in the last 2 seconds of each reel, and a bio that leads with the outcome (“evidence-based strength coaching for perimenopause”). And when 400 lands — post about it. “400 smart women” is a story.

Evidence: conversion ~0.2% during spike weeks vs ~2%+ in quiet weeks · audience 398

Facebook: holding at 153 through the quiet stretch

The page kept its new follower (153) while views wound down with the drought (2,495 → 293). The creatine (523) and achy-knees (379) videos remain its best ever.

Do: keep the auto-crosspost exactly as is — when the restart happens, Facebook comes along for free. Supplement and joint-health topics are what travel here.

Evidence: FB followers 153 · views 2,495 → 293 · interactions 11 → 0

Threads: engage, don’t just broadcast

Daily posting is not being distributed — most posts get under 20 views, and the “comment GUIDE” CTA posts get essentially none. The ones that traveled (the tea confession, 192 views; the cortisol explainer, 84) were personal or practical, not promotional.

Do: swap 3 of the 7 weekly posts for 10 minutes of replying to large women’s-health accounts — replies are how Threads distributes new accounts. Keep the protocol-style posts.

Evidence: 2 followers · weekly views 183 → 16 · posting paused after Jul 19

YouTube Shorts is free reach you’re leaving off

X (2 followers, one tweet, ~0 impressions) and YouTube (1 subscriber, no uploads) are dormant. X can stay parked. YouTube can’t: you already produce a vertical video every day, and Shorts is the only one of these surfaces where content keeps earning search views for months.

Do: add YouTube Shorts to the same Metricool cross-post. Zero new production, evergreen upside.

Evidence: YT 3 video views in 14 days with no uploads · X followers 3 → 2
